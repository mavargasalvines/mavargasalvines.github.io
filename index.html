About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Virginia Tech. My research lies at the intersection of labor economics, the economics of education, and applied microeconometrics, with a focus on the school-to-work transition and on how policy shapes labor markets and educational outcomes in the United States and Peru.

I am on the 2026–27 academic job market and expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027. My job market paper, Internships and the School-to-Work Transition, studies how undergraduate internships relate to graduates’ first jobs and starting salaries.

I also teach economics at Virginia Tech and the Universidad de Piura. More about my research, teaching, and CV is available on this site.
